Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Saarthak Maharana and what kind of projects do you take?

I’m a 3D environment artist from Delhi, India, crafting immersive worlds for games, cinematics and virtual experiences. I take on full environment builds — from blockout and kit creation to lighting, dressing and final optimization — in Unreal Engine 5 and Blender.

How long does a complete environment take?

A focused diorama or single hero scene usually ships in one to two weeks. Larger playable levels with custom kits, LODs and optimization typically run three to six weeks, depending on scope, assets and feedback rounds.

Do you deliver game-ready, optimized environments?

Yes — every scene follows real-time budgets: modular kits, trimmed draw calls, LODs, collision setup and in-engine profiling, so the world runs as smoothly on target hardware as it looks in screenshots.

What tools and standards do you work with?

Unreal Engine 5 (Lumen, Nanite, Sequencer), Blender, Substance 3D, Quixel Megascans and Adobe Premiere Pro — following PBR workflows, consistent texel density and clean, documented project structures your team can pick up instantly.

How do we start working together?

Send a brief — or even a rough idea — through the contact links below. I’ll reply with a plan, timeline and quote within 48 hours, then we move from blockout to final world in clear weekly milestones.
